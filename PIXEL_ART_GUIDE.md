## 🎨 Sprite Requirements

For your game, you need:

### Player (16x16 or 32x32 pixels)
- Facing different directions (optional for now)
- Idle animation (optional)

### Enemies (16x16 or 32x32 pixels)
- Normal enemy (red)
- Fast enemy (orange) 
- Tank enemy (purple/larger)

### Items (16x16 pixels)
- Health potion/heart
- Maybe coins for future use

### Tileset (16x16 tiles)
- Floor tiles
- Wall tiles
- Doors (for future rooms)

---

## 💾 Recommended Format

- **Format:** `.png` with transparency
- **Size:** 16x16, 32x32, or 64x64 pixels
- **Naming:** Clear names like `player.png`, `enemy_fast.png`

---

## 🎮 Using Sprites in Godot

Once you have sprite files:

1. **Import:** Drag `.png` files into `sprites/` folder
2. **Set filter:** Click imported sprite → Import tab → Reimport (this makes pixels crisp, not blurry)
3. **Replace nodes:** I'll help you swap Polygon2D → Sprite2D
